ARCHITECTURAL & FLIGHT MANUAL — AIR SUPERIORITY

1. TERMINAL TOPOLOGY & ROUTE SYNERGY

Every match drafts one of three bespoke terminal archetypes — Central Hub & Spokes, Dual Concourse Y-Shape, or Pier Ring / Satellite — each with 16 interconnected gates.

  • Route Synergy: Every directly connected corridor between two gates of the same airline compounds that gate's output by +25% (×1.25 per link, multiplying). Contiguous chains pay enormous dividends.
  • Pulsing Flight Paths: Connected gates you control light up as vibrant pulsing routes in your livery colour, so territory control is readable at a glance.
  • Pan & Zoom: Drag with one finger or mouse anywhere on the blueprint to pan the camera. Pinch with two fingers, scroll wheel, or use the on-screen + / − / 100% buttons to zoom.
  • 3D Gate Pins: Claimed gates display high-contrast 3D pins embossed with the carrier's emblem. Tap or hover over any pin to inspect owner and passenger throughput.
  • Commuter Agents: Watch 1950s UPA-style animated commuters walk between connected gates and react to one another with micro-interactions!

2. DUAL WIN CONDITIONS

  • Sudden Death (Gate Majority): Any carrier that controls a strict majority of all gates on the board (9 or more of the 16 gates) achieves immediate terminal monopoly and wins on the spot.
  • Turn Limit (Passenger Supremacy): If no airline achieves a 9-gate majority before the final round concludes (20 or 30 rounds), the carrier with the highest total passengers wins.

3. TURN STRUCTURE — ONE ACTION PER TURN

Turns proceed sequentially: Active Captain ➩ Rival Syndicate / Next Captain ➩ World Bulletin.

  • Automatic Boarding: At the start of a carrier's turn, every gate it controls boards passengers, multiplied by Route Synergy.
  • Automatic Draw: The active carrier then draws exactly 1 Flight Coupon. Hands are capped at 3 coupons; drawing a 4th automatically voids the oldest coupon in hand (FIFO).
  • Exactly One Action: Claim one unclaimed gate or deploy one Flight Coupon — never both. Choosing one immediately locks out the other for that turn.
  • Pass / End Turn: If no action can or will be taken, hit PASS TURN (or the Spacebar) to hand control to the next carrier.

4. THE TWELVE FLIGHT COUPONS

  • Gate Change: Convert one opponent-controlled gate to your airline.
  • Runway Maintenance: Close any gate — passenger generation disabled for 2 full rounds.
  • Lounge Upgrade: Permanently double one of your gates' base passenger output.
  • Charter Buyout: Instantly claim any unclaimed gate with an immediate bonus boarding wave.
  • Baggage Strike: Siphon 25% (max 400) of a rival carrier's accumulated passengers.
  • Direct Flight Charter: Immediate lump sum of +150 passengers.
  • Terminal Expansion: Spawn 1 new neutral gate wired into the nearest corridor.
  • FAA Audit: Every opposing carrier discards their oldest coupon.
  • Holding Pattern: Freeze a target gate for 1 round and siphon +90 passengers from delayed traffic.
  • Supersonic Jet: Permanently boost one of your gates by +60 base PAX and board an instant supersonic wave.
  • Smear Campaign: Sabotage a rival carrier's PR — drain 20% of their passengers and void their newest coupon.
  • Hostile Takeover: Seize an opponent's non-shielded gate and immediately collect 100% of its base passenger yield.

5. WORLD WIRE & RIVAL SYNDICATES

  • World Deck Events: Drawn every 3 rounds, impacting all carriers simultaneously (e.g., Pilot Shortage halves capacity; Holiday Rush doubles output).
  • Easy Syndicate: Picks opportunistic gates and occasionally overlooks stronger coupon plays.
  • Normal Syndicate: Evaluates every legal move — gate claim versus each coupon — and commits to the strongest play.
  • Hard Syndicate: Ruthless strategic calculation weighted toward blocking and sabotaging the leading carrier.
